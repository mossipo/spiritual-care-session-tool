PAUSE TO PRAY

Before you discern

Before you weigh what you have heard, ask the Spirit for discernment. Then be quiet together for a moment.

Suggested prayer

Use your own words, or start here.

Holy Spirit, You lead us into all truth (John 16:13). Show us what is really happening in name's life: what is of the flesh, what is a wound, and what, if anything, is of the enemy. Keep us from seeing what is not there, and from missing what is. Let anything we sense honour Jesus and agree with Your Word. In Jesus' name, amen.

Holy Spirit, what do You want to show us?

Impressions are weighed, not obeyed. Test them by the seven tests below, and with the others present (1 Cor 14:29), before acting on any of them.

The seven tests. The form of a thing is not its identity. Test the source, not the spectacle.

Tests over time vs. tests in the roomThe Christological, Scripture, self-control, and edification tests can be applied now. The fruit and life-trajectory tests need weeks and months. Mark what you can; leave the rest for follow-up.

Your pastoral assessment

When you discern a demonic element and the person is willing, this is the five-step pattern. Walk it; do not rush it. The house must be filled.

What not to do

No drama or theatrics. No extended conversation with a spirit; command, do not converse. Not in public; protect dignity. Never alone; minister as a team. Do not assume every problem is demonic, and do not create fear or fascination with demons.

In Jesus' name, amen.
