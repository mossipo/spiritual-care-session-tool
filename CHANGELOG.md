## v4.1: Prayer pauses (2026-11-03)

Seven places to stop and pray, each with a suggested prayer (folded away; "use your own words" comes first), a listening prompt, and a place to note what you sensed.

1. **Before they arrive** (Home, just you): heart searching, wisdom, discerning of spirits, covering, agreeing together.
2. **Opening** (Intake): ask permission first; welcome the Holy Spirit; peace and a safe place.
3. **When there is a safety concern** (Safety, appears only when a risk is marked): act on safety first, then pray alongside it.
4. **Before you discern** (Discern): ask for discernment and be quiet together. A reminder that impressions are weighed, not obeyed (1 Cor 14:29).
5. **Listen before you pray** (Ministry): ask if they are ready, then listen. Prayers of authority stay within the five steps.
6. **Sending** (Plan): the blessing of Numbers 6:24-26, filling, and prayer over their steps.
7. **After they leave** (Plan, just you): release the person to God, rest, and follow-up.

- Notes from every pause go into the confidential record under "Prayer and listening," never on the handout.
- Notes save with the file; a new session with the same person starts with fresh prayer notes.
- Menu: "Suggested prayers" shows or hides the written prayers (remembered on that device). The listening spaces always stay.
- Learn: new foundation lesson, "Praying through a session."
- Spirits are addressed only within the five steps of ministry, never in the opening or sending prayers.

## v4.0: New layout (2026-11-03)

The whole tool now uses the layout and type of Akouō. Everything it did before still works the same way.

**Layout**
- Home screen with three doors: New session, Continue (open a locked file), and Client intake. A "Return to" card appears when a session is open.
- On a phone: a slim top bar (person and session number, save status, Learn, Menu) and a fixed bottom bar with Back, the current step, and Next. Tap the step name to see all steps and jump to any of them.
- On a laptop: the steps sit across the top bar.
- Menu holds Save and lock, Open a saved file, New session (same person), Passphrase, Learn, Home, and Clear.
- On the last step, Next becomes "Save and lock."
- Save status always visible: Unsaved, Saved and locked, or Saved, not locked.

**Easier to read and use**
- Alegreya type at a larger size; fields at 16px so an iPhone no longer zooms in when you tap one.
- Dark mode follows the device setting. Printing is always light.
- Seven tests use green, amber and red buttons, the same as Weigh a Word in Akouō.
- Safety and Discern show a live result box that changes colour as you mark; Safety shows your referral line when a risk is marked.
- Triage "what happens next" box is colour-coded.
- Doors fold into groups that show how many items are marked.
- Plan offers two large choices: full plan or handout.

**Learn**
- The long "How to use this section" boxes moved into a Learn page: five foundation lessons (the seven tests, possession or oppression, fill the house, generational patterns, when to refer) and a lesson for each step.
- Each step now opens with a short guide card that can be hidden to a small bar. The choice is remembered on that device.

**Install and offline**
- Can be added to a phone, tablet or laptop home screen and opens without signal (from the web address).
- Session files are never stored by the app; they stay wherever you save them.
- index.html is still one self-contained file, so a downloaded copy works anywhere.

## v3.1: Passphrase-locked files (2026-11-03)

- Saved session files are now locked with a passphrase (AES-256 encryption, done on the device). A locked file shows only scrambled text to anyone without the passphrase.
- The first save asks you to choose a passphrase (at least 8 characters; a short sentence works well). Later saves in the same sitting lock with it automatically.
- Opening a locked file asks for its passphrase. A wrong passphrase is refused and the file stays closed.
- "Passphrase" button in the toolbar shows whether one is set and lets you change it.
- Older unlocked files still open; the next save locks them.
- Client intake: locking is optional. If the person locks their answers, they tell the passphrase to the minister in person.
- There is no way to recover a forgotten passphrase. Keep it somewhere safe and separate from the files.
- Save and load messages now appear as short notices instead of pop-up boxes.

## v3: Family genogram (2026-11-03)

**Family genogram on the Intake tab**
- Guided questions: nine ask-aloud questions covering the person, marriage, children, siblings, parents, and both sets of grandparents. The diagram draws itself as the person answers.
- Edit a person: tap anyone on the diagram to add great-grandparents, aunts, cousins, remarriages, or adopted children.
- Pattern chips on each person: faith, addiction, occult, abuse, sexual sin, breakdown, early death, illness, mental health.
- Pattern lens highlights everyone carrying a pattern; optional relationship lines (close, distant, conflicted, cut off, abusive).

**Pattern findings**
- Counts blood relatives only. People who married in are listed separately as part of their children's line.
- Shows how many generations a pattern spans, whether it skips a generation, and whether it runs sideways among siblings or cousins.
- Adopted people show with brackets, with a place for what is known of the birth family.
- "Mark on Doors tab" button for patterns repeating across generations. Nothing is marked unless the minister presses it.
- Mental-health and illness patterns point to medical care and referral, with prayer for healing alongside medical care (Jas 5:14-15).

**Prayer and theology**
- "Pause and listen" step closes the genogram questions; "Listen before you pray" added to the Ministry tab.
- Generational prayer path reordered: forgive the line, confess, repent, break the pattern, take authority only where a spirit is discerned. Added Exodus 20:6, Ezekiel 18:20, Leviticus 26:40-42, and Jeremiah 14:20.
- Guilt versus effects stated on the Doors tab, in the findings, and in the report: guilt is not inherited; effects carry on where a pattern repeats; God's love reaches a thousand generations.
- Handout: "A heritage to build on" when faith runs in the family; "The pattern can end with you" when generational work was done. No names or diagram on the handout.

**Records**
- Genogram, findings, and listening notes print in the confidential record only.
- Genogram carries forward to every new session with the same person.
- v2 session files still open, with an empty genogram ready to fill.
